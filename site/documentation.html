Methodology

Scientific review description. Archive citation, individual authorship, institutional terms and DOI remain under review.

Source variables and allocation

The canonical variable definitions use 2020 Census Demographic and Housing Characteristics (DHC) tabulations. Frozen SOURCE_AUTHORITY_MANIFEST.csv records direct-official DHC source and canonical-master hashes for all 52 state/territory components. Counts are associated with Census-block source geography. Retained historical block/fishnet geometry and the historical TIGER/Line 2022 AREAWATER family underpin spatial allocation; not every literal historical input/runtime state survived.

The recovered method constructs geodesic block-land density and subtracts water using the documented AREAWATER inputs. Block/grid intersections are aggregated by PIXELID. ArcGIS Pro 3.7.1 Intersect, Statistics and CELL_CENTER PolygonToRaster implement the retained 0.00416666665-degree grid workflow. The historical positive-land eligibility and round(PIXEL_LAND,5)>0 rule remain unchanged. The frozen HISTORICAL_ALLOCATION_PARAMETERS.json and FROZEN_INTERSECT_VARIANT.json carry exact parameters rather than this narrative replacing them.

Count values allocate block counts to eligible grid-cell land intersections. Fractional counts are expected. PIXEL_LAND and PIXEL_WATER carry allocated area in km² per cell; they are not count classes or independent census counts. No area or count grid is converted to density here. Pixel area varies with latitude and the source land/water geometry. Source ALAND20/AWATER20 in block polygons use m² and have a different role.

Corrected release

AUND1, A25OV, A65TO79, OCC and OWN were corrected across all applicable components. New Mexico, Puerto Rico, Wisconsin, Wyoming and Alaska have full 29-count reconstruction under frozen dispositions; the other states retain their documented mixed retained/re-executed surfaces. There are 380 corrected production jobs. FEMW18CHILD is excluded, BLACK is canonical, and the corrected dictionary governs older naming/formula conflicts.

Alaska Path B reconstructs block-land and pixel-land coherently from the complete frozen official TIGER2022 AREAWATER archive set and the recovered algorithm. It does not recover the missing literal historical assembled water state exactly. Local differences, including Kvichak Bay, remain documented. No water feature was removed and no reference grid patched to force agreement. Individual geographic coverage, dateline treatment and full archived masks follow the frozen Alaska decisions.

Aggregation and domains

National rasters/arrays follow the frozen national assembly and reconciliation rules. A national product is a composite representation, not another independent component to add to the 52 component selections. State bounding grids can overlap; valid-cell counts and masks must not be summed naively. Frozen national reconciliation tests sums with their existing numerical tolerance and separate TIFF/NetCDF identities. No source cells are removed for website cartography. National preview omissions of remote islands do not apply to the scientific archive.

Validation policy

Normal source-conservation screening uses relative residual <=0.0002 or absolute residual <=1 count. Exceptional acceptance requires deterministic unchanged-method allocation accounting, valid source and land-cache fingerprints, applicable structural/mask checks, and agreement with the expected allocated total under its frozen bound. This is not arbitrary redistribution of unexplained loss.

The final PIXEL_LAND v4 policy retains exact structure/mask and provenance checks, 1 ppm signed state-total and summed-absolute bounds, 5 m² RMSE, and local differences bounded by both 0.5% of geodesic cell area and 1,000 m². Older stricter results remain diagnostics. Mixed-surface canonical identities use 0.01 + 1e-8*abs(left) counts; single-execution/full-29 identities retain 1e-8 + 1e-10*abs(left). These approved scopes and thresholds were not changed for archival preparation. Read the original policy JSON and matrices for complete definitions.

Geospatial metadata

Scientific review description. Archive citation, individual authorship, institutional terms and DOI remain under review.

All freshly inspected raster and vector products declare WGS 84 / EPSG:4326. Raster x/y coordinates are longitude/latitude in decimal degrees; x increases eastward, y decreases southward in the inspected grids. GeoTIFF AREA_OR_POINT is Area. Pixel-center coordinates in each NetCDF agree with the corresponding TIFF transform. CF-1.8 is the declared NetCDF convention; this inspection is not a separate full CF-compliance certification.

Nominal raster spacing is 15 arc-seconds (1/240 degree); stored transforms are approximately 0.00416666665 degrees, with small floating-point representation differences. Cell ground size and area vary with latitude. Do not describe the whole release as fixed 500-m cells. Read geotransforms and coordinate variables instead of assigning a new grid.

NoData is format/geography-specific: 1.79e308 in all 31 Alaska GeoTIFFs; -inf in the other 1,612 GeoTIFFs; -1.7976931348623157e+308 in NetCDF scientific variables. Use rasterio masked reads or the declared NetCDF _FillValue. Valid zero is a real modeled value and is never converted to NoData. The positive Alaska sentinel must not be treated as an extreme valid observation.

Raster/NetCDF values are Float64. Polygon DBF canonical count fields are integer-valued numeric attributes; block ALAND20/AWATER20 use m². Shapefiles are polygons, not raster-cell geometries, and have no PIXEL_LAND/PIXEL_WATER attributes. All five associated components and their declared UTF-8 encoding are needed for reliable use.

Known limitations

Scientific review description. Archive citation, individual authorship, institutional terms and DOI remain under review.

Allocated grid counts are modeled spatial estimates, may be fractional, and do not constitute Census-published observations at cell level. Spatial resolution is angular. Land/water allocation and masks inherit the frozen historical eligibility, geometry and coverage semantics.

The expected unallocated values are exact recorded decimal strings, not rounded substitutions or authorized additional allocations. Acceptance requires deterministic accounting and agreement with the expected allocated result; counts are not forced into other cells. Full per-job residuals and bounds remain in RASTER_JOB_RESULTS_MANIFEST.csv.

A direct-official canonical zero can replace a null in a known-bad retained field, causing a documented zero-valued candidate-only mask extension. This narrow exception does not permit missing/nonzero discrepancies or arbitrary mask editing. Count-identity evaluation uses the frozen union-of-valid-cells/NoData-as-mathematical-zero convention only within its authorized scope; users should preserve the actual data masks.

Alaska Path B is a coherent reconstruction from official frozen TIGER2022 water sources, with localized differences including Kvichak Bay. The literal historical assembled input state was not recovered exactly. Historical input/runtime losses prevent a claim of universal bitwise reproduction. Practical PIXEL_LAND v4 equivalence passes coexist with older strict diagnostic failures; those failures are not concealed.

Five-variable states mix retained and re-executed surfaces under their separate approved identity bound. Full-29 states retain strict identity scope. National/component aggregation must follow the frozen masks, dateline domain and reconciliation evidence; do not add overlapping grid cell counts or include the national composite twice.

Historical documents can contain obsolete formulas, 500-m shorthand, year/citation placeholders and naming errors. The corrected canonical dictionary controls. FEMW18CHILD is excluded and BLACK is canonical. Archived source-text scripts may contain historical names or phases; they are evidence, not permission to rerun a paused pipeline.

The website is generalized cartography. Its national display can omit remote islands under a display-only policy; the archived scientific arrays and full-resolution statistics retain their source coverage. Preview pixels are not suitable for numerical analysis.

Rights, institutional attribution, final citation and external archive identities remain pending. The documented scientific exceptions remain part of the release interpretation.

Validation and reconciliation

The following national note is retained from the approved Science & Methods page. Numerical values and scientific qualifications are unchanged.

The national allocated population sum differs from the corresponding 2020 Census total by approximately 0.0005%. See the reconciliation documentation for details.

National allocated TOTALPOP is 334,733,492.4976411, compared with the corresponding 2020 Census benchmark of 334,735,155. The difference is approximately 1,662.5 persons (0.00049666%). National and component allocated totals reconcile with one another. The residual relative to the Census benchmark has no uniquely established cause; it is not definitively attributed to rounding.

National TOTALPOP has 62,967,001 valid cells; the component valid-cell counts sum to 63,055,358. Their histograms are not interchangeable. The existing national class-value sums were calculated independently from the national rasters, not by adding component class histograms.

The frozen validation/NATIONAL_RECONCILIATION.csv records zero national-versus-component allocated-total residual. The retained source-conservation documentation records eligibility and allocation-loss qualifications; it does not uniquely apportion the entire national benchmark difference.
